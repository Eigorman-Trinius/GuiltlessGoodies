Our Story

Guiltless Goodies was created with one mission: to bring back indulgence without the sugar spike.

We believe treats should feel fun, comforting, and delicious, not restrictive or complicated. That is why we developed two signature flour systems that let you choose your experience without compromising your health.

Our Guiltless Low Spike Blend is crafted from almond and coconut flour to deliver ultra low carb, low glycemic treats that taste like the real thing. For those who crave a classic bakery feel, our Slightly Guilty Blend combines oat and almond flour for a soft, nostalgic crumb with a better for you twist.

Every cookie is made entirely to order. You choose your flavor, your flour blend, your binder (vegan or classic), and the size that fits your moment. Nothing sits on a shelf. Every Guiltless Goody is made fresh, with quality ingredients, for genuinely great taste.

Guiltless Goodies is indulgence, reimagined. Treats that love you back.
