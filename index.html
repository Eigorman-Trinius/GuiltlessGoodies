Choose Your Binder

Every Guiltless Goodies treat is made to order — including your binder. Whether you keep it classic or fully plant-based, we've got you covered.

🌱 Vegan

Aquafaba Powder & Refined Coconut Oil

Our vegan option swaps two ingredients: aquafaba powder (a concentrated, dried chickpea water) replaces eggs, and refined coconut oil replaces butter. The result is a fully plant-based cookie with the same great texture and zero animal products.

  • Fully vegan, egg-free & dairy-free
  • Aquafaba powder binds just like eggs
  • Refined coconut oil has a neutral flavor — no coconut taste
  • Same great texture, no compromise
  • Perfect for plant-based lifestyles
🥚 Classic

Eggs & Butter

Whole eggs and real butter bring a rich, classic bakery structure — golden lift, that familiar chew, and the texture you know and love from a proper homemade cookie.

  • Rich, traditional texture
  • Classic bakery-style chew, lift & richness
  • The time-tested combination

Our Story

Guiltless Goodies was created with one mission: to bring back indulgence without the sugar spike.

We believe treats should feel fun, comforting, and delicious — not restrictive or complicated. That’s why we developed two signature flour systems that let you choose your experience without compromising your health.

Our Low‑Spike Blend is crafted from almond and coconut flour to deliver ultra low‑carb, low‑glycemic treats that taste like the real thing. For those who crave a classic bakery feel, our Slightly-Guilty Blend combines oat and almond flour for a soft, nostalgic crumb with a better‑for‑you twist.

Every flavor, every size, every product follows a simple structure: Flavor → Flour Blend → Size. It keeps things clean, intuitive, and endlessly customizable.

Guiltless Goodies is indulgence, reimagined — treats that love you back.
