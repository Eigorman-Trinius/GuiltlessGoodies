Indulgence Without the Spike

Real bakery treats made entirely to order, built around your life. Every Guiltless Goody starts with a thoughtful system: choose your flour blend for the carb profile you want, your binder for the lifestyle you live (vegan or classic), and your size for exactly how much you want to indulge.

Why Guiltless Goodies Works

Diabetic Friendly by Design

Every flour blend we use is chosen specifically to keep net carbs low and blood sugar steady. No wheat, no refined flour — just smart ingredients that let you indulge without the spike.

Naturally Gluten Free

Our blends are built entirely from almond, coconut, and oat flour — naturally gluten free, no substitutions needed. Whether you avoid gluten by choice or necessity, every treat on our menu works for you.

Vegan Available on Every Treat

Every Guiltless Goody can be made fully plant-based. Choose aquafaba powder or flax egg as your vegan binder, paired with refined coconut oil in place of butter — same great texture and flavor, zero animal products, no separate menu required.

Choose Your Binder

Every Guiltless Goodies treat is made to order, including your binder. Whether you keep it classic or fully plant-based, we have got you covered.

🌱 Vegan

Vegan Binder Options

Every Guiltless Goody can be made fully plant-based. Choose your vegan binder — both eliminate eggs entirely and pair with refined coconut oil in place of butter.

  • Aquafaba Powder — concentrated dried chickpea water that binds just like eggs, neutral flavor, seamless texture
  • Flax Egg — ground flaxseed mixed with water, adds a subtle nuttiness and a hearty, wholesome chew
  • Both are fully vegan, egg-free & dairy-free
  • Refined coconut oil replaces butter with no coconut taste
  • Same great texture, no compromise
🥚 Classic

Eggs & Butter

Whole eggs and real butter bring a rich, classic bakery structure with golden lift, that familiar chew, and the texture you know and love from a proper homemade cookie.

  • Rich, traditional texture
  • Classic bakery-style chew, lift & richness
  • The time-tested combination

Zero Glycemic. Zero Compromise.

Zero-Glycemic Sweeteners, Chosen Per Product

Every Guiltless Goody skips sugar entirely. Our cookies use Lakanto Monkfruit + Erythritol (Classic and Golden) for zero-spike sweetness with perfect crisp browning. Our muffins and breads use Monkfruit + Allulose — a rare sugar that keeps baked goods tender and moist with the same zero glycemic impact.
